# Exit Code scenarios
#
# One scenario per line. A line starting with "## " starts a group in the
# host's scenario picker. Other lines starting with "#" are comments, and blank
# lines are ignored. Keep each scenario under 240 characters.
#
# When the host doesn't pick scenarios, every round draws one at random from
# this whole file. Redeploy after editing (npm run deploy) to update the live game.

## Computer club
The server room is flooding, the door is jammed, and the only backup of the school website is on the top rack.
A robotics club Roomba has gained sentience and locked you in the supply closet. It is charging up.
Your laptop battery is at 1% and the AI controlling the school lockdown needs your password typed in 30 seconds.
A swarm of drones from the drone-racing team has mistaken you for the finish gate.
The 3D printer in the lab is printing an endless staircase and you are standing on the last step.
You fell asleep in the computer lab and woke up locked in after hours. The motion-sensor sprinklers just activated.
A rogue chatbot has taken over the PA system and challenged you to a debate. Losers get recycled.
You plugged in a mysterious USB drive and every screen in the building now shows a countdown timer.
The robotics competition robot has locked onto you as the target and the remote has no batteries.
The school Wi-Fi router has overheated, caught fire, and is the only thing between you and the exit.
You are stuck in a crashed VR headset and the simulation is set to "volcano".
Your group project partner uploaded your consciousness into a spreadsheet. Someone is about to press delete on row 7.
The AI tutor has decided the final exam is now a game of chess, and every lost piece is a lost limb.
A power surge fused your hands to the keyboard just as the overheating PC starts to smoke.
The smart fridge in the teachers' lounge has locked you inside and set itself to deep freeze.
An escaped class lizard has chewed through the main power cable and you are holding both ends.
You are on a rooftop during a thunderstorm holding the only metal antenna that restores the school network.
The self-driving golf cart from the tech fair is driving you straight toward the lake and the brakes are a CAPTCHA.
Someone ran a deletion command on the computer that controls the school's heating. It is January, and the doors just locked.
The LAN party extension cords are plugged into each other fourteen deep, and the carpet has started to glow.
Your code compiled on the first try. The universe has flagged this as a glitch and sent something to fix it.
The club's AI art generator escaped into the hallway and is turning everyone it sees into a drawing. It just saw you.

## Classic peril
You're trapped in a sinking submarine with 60 seconds of oxygen left.
The cafeteria vending machine has tipped forward and you're pinned underneath it. It's still dispensing.
You're stranded on a melting ice floe with a hungry polar bear and a spork.
A great white shark is circling your inflatable flamingo, 500 meters from shore.
You wake up in a coffin buried six feet underground with a phone at 3% battery and no signal.
An avalanche is roaring down the mountain toward your ski lodge hot tub.
You are falling from a plane at 10,000 feet and your parachute is a backpack full of library books.
A volcano is erupting and the only way off the island is a raft made of pool noodles.
You are in a canoe at the top of a waterfall and your paddle just floated away.
A zombie horde has surrounded the mall. You are in the food court with a smoothie blender.
You are lost in the desert at noon with no water, no shade, and a very confident GPS.
Quicksand is up to your waist and the only thing nearby is a suspicious-looking vine.
A tornado is lifting your house off the ground with you still inside the bathtub.
You are stuck at the top of a broken Ferris wheel during a lightning storm.
A bear has wandered into your campsite and is very interested in your tent full of snacks.
You are trapped in a giant maze, a minotaur is getting closer, and you dropped the string.
Your hot air balloon is losing altitude fast over a field of cacti.
An angry swarm of bees has chased you onto a narrow rope bridge over a canyon.
You are locked in a bank vault that will open in 48 hours. There is air for 4.
A giant tidal wave is heading for the beach and you are in the middle of a sandcastle contest.
You are the last person on a sinking cruise ship and the lifeboats are all gone.

## Space
Your spacecraft is spinning out of control and the manual is written in a language you do not speak.
You are floating untethered 30 meters from the space station, and your thruster pack is out of fuel.
The ship's computer has decided you are a stowaway and will open the airlock in 60 seconds.
A meteor shower is heading for your Mars greenhouse, and the potatoes are not harvested yet.
Your lunar rover's battery died 10 kilometers from base, and the sun sets in 20 minutes.
The space station's oxygen recycler is broken, and the only spare part is inside the last pudding cup.
You landed on an alien planet where the locals only communicate through interpretive dance. They look hungry.
